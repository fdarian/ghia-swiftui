Each real glass is one `NSGlassEffectView`, one container view and one compositing layer (18 on the `button` demo, 4 on `syne-main-window`). Nothing above a `NativeGlass` has `opacity`, animated or static: the group it forms is drawn before the glass layer, so the glass refracts the children and the group's bounds cut the shadow square.
